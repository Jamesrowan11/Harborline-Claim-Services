# Employee Manual

## Daily flow
1. **Dashboard** — your open tasks, overdue items, and pipeline snapshot.
2. **Task Center** — work "Mine" first, then "Overdue".
3. **Leads** — new website inquiries arrive here with research tasks
   pre-created. Check the duplicate panel before doing anything else.
4. **Cases** — the case page is the single source of truth: overview,
   claimants, documents, communications, financials, tasks, sources, notes.

## Working a lead
- Verify property and sale details from public records; log each as a Source
  Record with a staleness date.
- If it's a duplicate, mark it (the audit trail records everything).
- When research supports it, **Convert to Case** — the case number,
  property, and claimant records are created automatically.

## Stages and language
Move cases along the pipeline with the stage selector; add a reason when it
helps the next person. Clients only ever see the client-facing label. In all
writing: funds are "possible" until the holder verifies them; never promise
recovery; never imply we are a government office, court, or law firm.

## Communications
Compose from an approved template only. Drafts require approval, and sending
passes the compliance gate (consent, opt-outs, verification level, frequency,
holds). If a send is blocked, the reason is shown — fix the underlying issue,
never work around it. Log calls and returned mail in the Communication Center.

## Documents
Upload through the case page (never email attachments). Mark a document
client-visible only when the client should see it. Pending documents need
review/approval by someone with that permission.

## Compliance quick rules
- No outreach before the case's verification level and compliance approval.
- Anything legally complex (probate, heirs, trusts, businesses, bankruptcy,
  disputes, minors, deceased owners) → set legal complexity accordingly; the
  system routes it to attorney review.
- A client asking us to stop contact is honored immediately — the portal and
  opt-out system enforce it, and you must never bypass it.
- Never request SSNs, bank logins, or card details.

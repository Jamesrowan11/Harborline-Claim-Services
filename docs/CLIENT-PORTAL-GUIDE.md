# Client Portal Help Guide

## Signing in
Use the "Client Login" link and the email address we set up with you. Forgot
your password? Use "Forgot your password?" and we'll email a secure reset
link.

## What you can do
- **See your case status** in plain language (for example "Documents Needed"
  or "Claim Submitted").
- **Upload requested documents** — when we need something, it appears at the
  top of your case with an Upload button. Files go over an encrypted
  connection and are never handled by regular email.
- **Message your case manager** — replies appear on the same page and we
  notify you by email when there's news.
- **Review and sign agreements** — read at your own pace; you're always
  welcome to have your own attorney review anything before you sign.
- **See payments** — when funds are distributed, the amounts appear on your
  case page.
- **Update your details** — email, phone, and mailing address under
  "My Details".
- **Control how we contact you** — stop text messages, ask us to stop all
  contact, or request account closure at any time under "My Details".

## Good to know
- We will never ask for your Social Security number, bank login, or card
  details through this portal's messages or by email.
- "Possible funds" means exactly that — nothing is guaranteed until the
  official funds holder makes its decision.
- If anything seems off — an email, a call, a letter — use the "Verify a
  Letter" page on our website or call the number published there.
